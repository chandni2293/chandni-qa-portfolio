Source-to-Target Data Validation


  • Compare source and target row counts.
  • Validate primary keys and customer IDs.
  • Check mandatory fields and NULL values.
  • Identify duplicate records.
  • Validate transformation rules.
  • Perform source-to-target reconciliation.
  • Validate rejected and successfully loaded records.

Example: Source contains 10,000 records and target contains 9,850 records. The 150-record difference should be investigated against the agreed business rules and rejection criteria.
